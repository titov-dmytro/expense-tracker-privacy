Delete your account

Expense Tracker · Last updated: 9 November 2026

You can ask us to delete your Expense Tracker account and everything in it at any time. This page explains what gets deleted and how to request it.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email from the address you registered with to info@finappventure.com.
  2. Use the subject line "Delete my Expense Tracker account".
  3. We will confirm the request and delete the data described below.

Email a deletion request

We normally complete deletion within 30 days, and we will email you when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in credentials
  • Your profile — email address, display name and preferences
  • All transactions you created, including amounts, stores, sectors and payment methods
  • All line items read from your receipts — product names, quantities and prices
  • All receipt images you uploaded
  • Any cards you recorded (last four digits and nickname)

If you are the owner of a group (a family or shared budget), tell us whether you want the group deleted too or transferred to another member.

What may be kept, and for how long

  • Backups. Receipt images may remain in automated backups for a short period before those backups expire on their own. They are not accessible through the app in the meantime.
  • Legal obligations. We may need to retain limited records if the law requires it.

Nothing is kept for advertising, and your data is never sold.

Deleting only some data

You do not need to delete your whole account to remove data. Inside the app you can delete individual transactions and their receipt images yourself, at any time, from the Transactions tab. That data is removed immediately.
