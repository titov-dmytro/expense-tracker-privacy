Privacy Policy — Expense Tracker

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Expense Tracker ("the app") is a personal expense-tracking application for Android. This policy explains what the app collects, why, where it is stored, and the choices you have.

The app is operated by an individual developer ("we", "us"). Contact: info@finappventure.com

1. Information we collect

Information you provide

DataWhy
Email addressTo create and sign in to your account
Display nameShown to you and to members of groups you join
Transactions — date, amount, currency, store name, spending sector, payment method (cash, or last 4 digits of a card)The core function of the app

Receipt imagesStored so you can retrieve a photo of a receipt later

Information collected automatically

  • Account identifier — an opaque ID issued by our authentication provider
  • Authentication tokens — stored on your device so you stay signed in

What the app does not collect

  • No advertising identifiers, no ad networks, no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs
  • No location data
  • No contacts, calendar, SMS or call logs
  • We do not collect your full card numbers. Only the last four digits you enter.

2. Receipt scanning happens on your device

Text recognition on receipt photos is performed entirely on your device, by text-recognition software built into the app. The image is not sent to us or to any third party for recognition.

Once a receipt is recognised, the app uploads a copy of that image to our storage so you can view it later from any device. You can delete a transaction and its image at any time.

3. Where your data is stored

WhatWhere

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest.

We rely on established third-party providers for cloud hosting, managed databases, file storage, authentication and email delivery. They act as our service providers (data processors): they may process your data only on our instructions and are bound by contract to protect it. We do not list every provider by name in this policy — you can ask us for the current list of service providers at any time (see section 10).

4. How your data is shared

  • With members of a group you join. If you add a transaction to a group, the other members of that group can see it. Personal transactions are visible only to you. Leaving a group removes your access to its data.
  • With our service providers (cloud hosting, storage, authentication and email delivery), solely to run the app.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not share it for advertising.

We may disclose data if legally required to do so.

5. Retention and deletion

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists.

To delete your account and all associated data, email info@finappventure.com from your registered address. We will delete your account, transactions, line items and receipt images, and will confirm when this is done. Deletion is normally completed within 30 days.

Note that receipt images may persist in automated backups for a limited period before those backups expire.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, or to object to its processing. Contact us at the address above and we will help. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

We use encryption in transit and at rest, least-privilege access controls, and automated backups. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. Material changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, announced in the app.
